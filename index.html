About

I study motivation, persistence, and collaborative learning in makerspaces and technology-enhanced learning environments. I use interaction analysis and mixed-method approaches to examine how learners navigate challenges during collaborative STEM activities. My broader interests include learning analytics and AI in education, with the aim of contributing to the design of learning environments that support meaningful learning.

My doctoral thesis, Characterizing Persistence as a Process in Collaborative Electronic Making: An Interactional Account, has been submitted. Supervised by Prof. Ramkumar Rajendran and co-supervised by Prof. Sridhar Iyer, it uses process analysis and qualitative methods to understand the interactional processes through which teams sustain productive persistence during extended making activities.

Persistence in Collaborative Making

OngoingDoctoral Research
2022 – present · IIT Bombay

My doctoral research investigates motivation and persistence in collaborative electronic making through video-based interaction analysis. It examines how learners navigate challenges during STEM design activities and collaborative problem solving.

Using process analysis and qualitative analysis, the work builds an understanding of the behavioural manifestations of persistence — productive persistence and wheel spinning — and identifies the interactional processes through which teams sustain productive persistence during extended making activities.

Supervisors

Prof. Ramkumar Rajendran and Prof. Sridhar Iyer, IIT Bombay.

Related output

ICLS 2025 — persistence in makerspaces ICLS 2024 — self-efficacy

Pi Jam and Maker Factory Ethnography

CompletedEthnography
2023 – 2024 · IIT Bombay

Three months of fieldwork across Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC) government schools in Mumbai, studying how low-cost makerspace programmes (Pi Jam and Maker Factory) are implemented for middle school learners.

Approach

Interviews with facilitators alongside classroom observations, analysed using thematic analysis to examine pedagogical practices, student engagement, gender inclusivity, and maker culture.

Supervisor

Prof. Ramkumar Rajendran, IIT Bombay.

Output

Findings presented as a poster at the 8th International Symposium on Academic Makerspaces (ISAM 2024), Sheffield, UK.

Online Inclusive Library for Deaf and Hard of Hearing

CompletedInclusive Design
2021 · IIT Bombay

Developed a Figma prototype of an online digital library for Deaf and Hard of Hearing (DHH) learners.

Created additional curricular resources including a picture book and home sign videos, integrated as core components of the application prototype for scientific concepts.

Conducted a study to evaluate the effectiveness of the prototype with target users, which affirmed its value — highlighting its significance in fostering inclusive education for DHH learners.

Supervisor
